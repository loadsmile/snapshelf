# SnapShelf Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

CodeCrafting, based in Portugal ("we", "us"), operates SnapShelf. This policy explains how the mobile app processes personal data. For privacy questions, contact **codecrafting.ltd@gmail.com**.

## Data we process

- **Account:** email address, account ID, optional display name, and authentication information processed by Supabase Auth. We do not receive your password by support email.
- **Content and organization:** titles, notes, labels, source URLs, favorites, archive status, Shelf and Stack names, Board positions, relationships, and timestamps. These records are stored in Supabase and associated with your account.
- **Installation and local media references:** a randomly generated installation ID and relative file paths associated with your account and device. These references sync as metadata; they do not contain image contents.
- **Images you select:** the app processes and copies images into its own storage on the device where you import them. The current app does not upload image contents to Supabase. Images are not synced by SnapShelf to other devices. Your device's operating-system backup or transfer features may handle app data separately; see **Device storage and backups**.
- **Service and support information:** Supabase may process network information (such as IP address), user agents, request timestamps, and operational logs to run and protect the service. If you email us, we process your message, address, and any details you provide to handle your request. Please do not email passwords or sensitive images.

The current app has no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-app tracking. Recheck this statement if the app or hosting changes.

## Why we process it and legal bases

We use account and content data to provide the features you request, synchronize metadata, authenticate you, process account changes and deletion, and respond to support requests. Our legal bases are performance of our agreement with you for account and core app features; legitimate interests in service security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and responding to support; and legal obligations where a law requires retention or disclosure. Supplying an email is needed to use an account; a display name and saved content are optional, although without saved content the organization features have nothing to display.

## Device storage and backups

SnapShelf stores imported images in its application document directory. It does not offer cloud image sync or restore. Images may be unavailable after uninstall, clearing app data, device loss, or changing devices. The Android app allows operating-system backup; its current configuration excludes SecureStore preferences from Android backup and transfer rules but **does not explicitly exclude app-local image files**. A device or cloud backup managed by the operating system may therefore include app data, subject to the platform, user settings, and backup limits. A backup or transfer does not make images available through SnapShelf account sync.

## Providers, recipients, and international transfers

Supabase provides authentication, database hosting, Realtime, storage infrastructure, and the account-deletion function. Email delivery and support-email services may process messages and associated information. Google Play, Vercel (which hosts these pages), and operating-system backup providers may process information when you use their respective services; they are not the SnapShelf account database. These providers may process information outside Portugal. To ask about the location of your data or international transfers, contact us at **codecrafting.ltd@gmail.com**. We may disclose information when legally required.

## Retention and deletion

We retain account data and synced content while your account is active. You may delete individual content in the app. To request deletion of your account and associated active backend records, use **Settings → Security → Delete account** or, even without the app, use the public [account-deletion page](../public-site/delete-account/index.html) to contact us by email. We may ask for information needed to verify that you own the account; never send your password. In-app deletion removes your account and associated active backend records and attempts to remove app-local image files on the device where you initiate it. An email request cannot remotely delete device-local images, including files on other devices.

Deletion does not necessarily remove residual temporary files, operating-system backups, support messages, provider logs, or database backups immediately. Backups and logs are retained according to the applicable provider's retention settings; support and deletion-request correspondence is retained as needed to handle requests and document their resolution. If a backup is restored, previously deleted data may reappear until the deletion is reapplied. Contact **codecrafting.ltd@gmail.com** to ask about a particular record or retention period.

## Security

We use access controls, HTTPS connections to the backend, and platform-protected native authentication storage. No system is completely secure. Do not send passwords or authentication tokens to support.

## Your rights
