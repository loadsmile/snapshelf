Delete your SnapShelf account and data

If you can sign in, open SnapShelf → Settings → Security → Delete account. Type DELETE and enter your current password. This requests deletion of your account and associated server-side content.

Request deletion without the app

Email codecrafting.ltd@gmail.com with the subject “SnapShelf Account Deletion Request” from the email associated with your account. If you cannot access that email, explain this in your request; we will need to verify that you own the account before deleting it. Do not send your password. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a request.

What is deleted

Deletion removes the account and its active server-side profile, Snap metadata, Shelves, Stacks, and relationships. In-app deletion also attempts to remove SnapShelf images on the device where you complete it. An email request cannot remove files stored on your device; uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove them, depending on your device and backups. Images stored on another device cannot be deleted remotely by SnapShelf. Provider logs and backups may remain for a limited period; see the privacy policy for the reviewed retention details before relying on it.

For further questions, contact codecrafting.ltd@gmail.com.
